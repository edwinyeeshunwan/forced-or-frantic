# Deviations from, and additions to, the frozen protocol

Protocol frozen 11 September 2026 (SHA-256 in `PROTOCOL_FREEZE.json`). The primary specification was not changed after freezing. The items below are implementation clarifications or clearly labelled additions. Primary results are reported exactly as the frozen protocol defines them.

| # | Type | When | Item |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clarification | during implementation, before any outcome was inspected | S6a/S6b recovery durations are measured from onset E0. Because window W starts at E0 − 15 min, the trough bar can precede E0, so a crossing before E0 would give a negative duration. Durations are floored at 0. |
| 2 | Clarification | during implementation | S11 single-asset regressions drop the ETH indicator, which is constant (collinear) within one asset. |
| 3 | Clarification | during implementation | S10 excludes onsets from 10 Oct 2025 00:00 to 18 Oct 2025 00:00 UTC ("10–17 October" read inclusively). The legacy grid used `between('2025-10-10', '2025-10-17')`, which ends at 17 Oct 00:00. |
| 4 | Presentation | after first run | The S12 continuous regressor is expressed per percentage point of OI destroyed (−100·ΔOI), so the coefficient is in log-return percentage points per percentage point. |
| 5 | Addition, not a sensitivity | after first run | The legacy event table is re-evaluated with the same dependence-aware inference (episode bootstrap, WCR, RMST to 1,440 min from the legacy onset). This separates the effect of the definitional corrections from the effect of the inference correction. It is labelled a comparison throughout. |
| 6 | Post-hoc diagnostic | after seeing S5a | Rank correlations between OI destroyed and the two liquidation-size measures, and class medians of those measures, were added (`size_diagnostics_posthoc`). This explains why the E2 coefficient depends on the size control. It is not used to choose a specification. |
| 7 | Presentation | after first run | Descriptive tables report KM share unrecovered and RMST at τ = 1,320 min instead of the largest observed duration. |
| 8 | Post-hoc diagnostic | after seeing S5a | A regression of \|displacement\| on continuous OI destroyed with the window-total size control (all analysable events, 6-h episodes, WCR). It asks whether the OI measure retains an association once contemporaneous liquidation volume is held fixed. It is reported only as a diagnostic. |
| 9 | Wording correction (no numbers changed) | 12 Sep 2026, after an independent review | The frozen protocol (section 1) says outcomes measured after the event window "begin after the classification information is available". That is exact for recovery, whose clock starts at A, but loose for the 6-h and 24-h price levels: their endpoints are later, yet they are measured against the pre-event reference and therefore still contain the within-window movement used to classify the event. The paper now states this, and makes no claim of incremental predictive power. |
| 10 | Wording correction (no numbers changed) | 12 Sep 2026, after an independent review | The frozen protocol (section 4) calls S9 "a bound-type check". S8 and S9 examine two selected assumptions; they do not bound the possible error from missing fills or removed rows. The paper and summary now say "probe sensitivity to selected assumptions". |

An independent reviewer checked the delivered v3 on 11 September 2026: all 26 tests, all 134 events and 41 saved columns, `primary_effects.json`, the legacy-definitions clustered effects, the unchanged originals and the non-image delivery hashes all reproduced, using pyarrow rather than the fallback reader. The review requested wording corrections only. Items 9 and 10 above, the four-month versus five-month sample distinction, softer absence-of-effect language where window volume is controlled, and explicit wording that episode independence is an assumption were applied to the paper and the plain-English summary on 12 September 2026. No estimate, interval, test or output file changed.
